সঠিক সময়ে, সঠিক সেবা।Right care, at the right time. A Bangla, voice-first guide from "what's wrong?" to "where do I go, and how fast?"

The mark

An H whose crossbar is a heartbeat. The two pillars are the person and the care they reach; the pulse is the moment HealthMax connects them. A single shapla-pink dot sits on the peak: the one warm accent, carried over from the hackathon build.

Rules

  • Clear space on every side: at least the width of one pillar ×2.
  • Minimum size: 24 px on screen; below that, use the glyph without the tile.
  • Never recolour the tile red, amber or green. Those colours mean urgency.
  • Never add a cross, stethoscope or robot to the mark.

Colour

Calm, warm and trustworthy. The brand never looks like an alarm, so that when red does appear, people take it seriously. The shipped hackathon build used a crimson primary almost identical to its emergency red; this palette fixes that.

Every pairing meets WCAG AA in light and dark mode. Urgency is never carried by colour alone: it always has a word and an icon.

Typography

Bangla first. Bangla glyphs carry more detail above and below the line, so Bangla text is set about 10% larger than Latin with roomier line height.

Pattern & icons

The pulse line, drawn as a kantha running stitch: in Bengali homes, kantha is how worn cloth is mended. Care that mends. Use it behind heroes and empty states at low opacity, never behind body text. Icons are outline, 1.75 px stroke, rounded, compatible with Lucide.

Designing for a worried person

People open a triage app when they are scared, often at night, often for someone else. Each rule below comes from that moment.

  • Answer first, explain later. Urgency and the next step lead; possible causes sit behind a tap, in gentle words. A ranked list of diseases with percentages feeds health anxiety.
  • One question per screen, with large yes/no buttons (48 px+) and a microphone everywhere.
  • Say what to do, not just how bad it is: "Go to a hospital that can do an ECG now", never just "Emergency".
  • Show where the answer came from: guideline, date, reviewed by. Trust is earned in the footnote.
  • Honest about uncertainty: "I'm not sure. A doctor should see this." beats false confidence.
  • Private by default for sexual, reproductive and mental-health questions.
  • Never "diagnosis". HealthMax gives guidance; only a doctor diagnoses.

Voice

Calm, plain, spoken Bangla, like a trusted elder sister who happens to be a nurse. Short sentences. No jargon, no fear, no false comfort.

Do

"আজই একজন ডাক্তার দেখান। জ্বর ৩ দিনের বেশি হলে রক্ত পরীক্ষা দরকার হতে পারে।"

Don't

"ফাইনাল ডায়াগনোসিস: ডেঙ্গু (৩৯.৯৯%)। প্যারাসিটামল খান।"
